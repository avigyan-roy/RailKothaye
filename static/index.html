THE IDEA BEHIND RAILETA

Predict the journey still to come.

A current delay is a snapshot. Our ETA considers each remaining section, the next halt, and the conditions ahead.

01

Receive data

Replay GPS pings and snap them to the train’s route, rejecting outliers.

02

Assess conditions

Account for congestion, temporary speed restrictions, fog, and stale GPS.

03

Predict remaining travel time

Use historical section medians and station dwells, respecting scheduled departures.

04

Update station ETAs

Show the prediction beside the baseline, with an uncertainty range and reasons.

Baseline = scheduled arrival + current delay
RailETA = now + remaining section travel + remaining station halts

All data is simulated. Backtests use unseen journey dates; these results do not establish real-world accuracy.
